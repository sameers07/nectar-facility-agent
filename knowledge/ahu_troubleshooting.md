# AHU Troubleshooting Guide

## Low airflow

If an AHU reports low airflow or a LOW_AIRFLOW alert, check the following
in order: (1) the return and supply air filters for clogging or excessive
dust loading, (2) the damper actuators to confirm they are commanding to
the correct open position, (3) the belt drive (on belt-driven fan units)
for slippage or wear, (4) the fan motor for correct RPM against its
nameplate rating. Filters are the most common cause and should be
inspected first — a clogged filter can reduce airflow by 30-50% before
any other symptom appears.

## High airflow / overpressure

Airflow above the design setpoint usually indicates a damper stuck open or
a control sequence fault. Verify the AHU's control sequence is in
automatic mode, not manual override, and check the building management
system for any active manual overrides left from prior maintenance.

## Unit not starting

Confirm the disconnect switch is in the ON position, check for tripped
breakers, and verify the start/stop control signal from the BMS. If the
unit has a safety interlock (smoke detector, freeze stat), confirm the
interlock has not tripped.

## When to escalate

Escalate to a maintenance service request if: airflow remains below 50%
of design after filter replacement, the fan motor draws current above its
nameplate rating, or the unit fails to start after confirming power and
control signals are present.
