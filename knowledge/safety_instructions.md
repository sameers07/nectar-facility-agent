# Safety Instructions

## Before manual intervention

Never open an AHU access panel or chiller enclosure without confirming
lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures have been followed and the unit's
disconnect is verified open. Rotating fan blades and electrical components
inside these units remain hazardous even when a unit appears stopped.

## Refrigerant safety

Chiller refrigerant leaks can displace oxygen in enclosed mechanical
rooms. If a refrigerant alarm is active, do not enter the mechanical room
-- ventilate first and follow the facility's refrigerant release protocol.

## Automated actions

This assistant must not perform any action that changes facility state
(such as creating or updating a service request) without explicit user
confirmation first, even when a fault is clearly identified.
